# OT6 v0.5 playtest brief

## Supported range

Play from the beginning through the complete Opera sequence.  The v0.5 stop
point is reached after Ultros is defeated, Setzer joins, and the Blackjack is
acquired.  Vector and the rest of the World of Balance are v0.6 work.

This is a playtest-first release.  The questions below matter more than
matching a speculative target number.

## Highest-value pass

1. Load a real v0.4 or v0.4.1 save near Zozo, then save and reload once.
   Open **X → Skills → SwdTech** and confirm Cyan's loadout starts in Auto
   mode rather than inheriting garbage from the old save.
2. Fight ordinary encounters on the route from Zozo through the Opera.
   Watch whether ability MP costs create useful resource decisions without
   turning the route into an Ether grind.  Check the displayed prices,
   unaffordable grey states, and refusal feedback for Blitz, Tools, Bushido,
   and Steal.
3. Configure Cyan's four Bushido slots.  Check whether L/R assignment, Y for
   Auto, slot-to-BP mapping, persistence after saving, and the fallback after
   spending Oblivion are understandable without outside explanation.
4. Complete the Jidoor and Opera sequence normally.  Note any cutscene stall,
   confusing aria choice, collision snag, timer problem, or unclear rafter
   switch feedback.
5. Fight Ultros.  Check whether the six-shield slash/pierce gauge is readable
   and reachable with the party you brought, whether the fight lasts long
   enough to break him, and whether its danger and MP pressure feel fair
   without Banon.
6. Continue until Setzer joins and the Blackjack is acquired.  Confirm the
   transition completes cleanly and the game returns to a stable,
   saveable/controllable state.

## Useful regression sampling

- Try a few fixed-party imperial encounters.  Every body should have a
  reachable physical break axis, but it should not always be the default
  attack.
- Revisit the Narshe school lesson and judge whether its "armor has seams"
  framing matches what combat now teaches.
- Open battle submenus with long names, insufficient MP, different pending BP
  amounts, and full/empty Bushido slots.  Look for cursor, cost-column,
  greying, font, or HUD corruption.
- If convenient, begin once from a fresh save as a control against the
  upgraded-save pass.

## Reporting

Please include the emulator or hardware, whether the save was fresh or
upgraded, the party and rough levels at the Opera, and a save or savestate
near any failure.  Screenshots are especially useful for menu or HUD defects.
