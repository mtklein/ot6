## What's changed

**Ribbons stay with the party in the World of Ruin saves.** When the party changes members on the Falcon, anyone left behind now hands over their Ribbon first, and it goes on someone in the new party. Before, a Ribbon could stay on a character waiting on the airship, and the party reached the Colosseum and Maranda with only one of its three Ribbons on the members it was using.

**Kefka's Tower has its own shields and weak points.** Every monster and boss in the tower now has shields and weapon weaknesses chosen for it. Swords don't break the shields of the machines in the tower's factory; spears, knives, clubs and lightning do, so the characters who sat out much of the World of Ruin have a part to play. Each of the three statues can be broken with cards or a brush, plus one other kind of weapon that differs from statue to statue. The Guardian you finally fight in the tower now shows a shield gauge. The Colosseum's opponents that come from the tower get the same new weak points.
