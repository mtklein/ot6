## What's changed

Four World of Balance monsters off the story's path now have shields designed
for them: the Bug and the ChickenLip in the forests by Albrook, the Grenade on
the bomb isle, and the Intangir on Triangle Island. The Bug breaks to blades
and spears, the ChickenLip to blades and fists. The Grenade explodes when a
Fight hits it without breaking it, so break it in one swing, or use Edgar's
tools and Sabin's blitzes, which it does not answer. The Intangir absorbs every
element, so a weapon that carries one heals it, but Locke's knife, Edgar's
tools, Sabin's blitzes and Setzer's cards and dice all break it. A broken
Intangir loses its turns, so it cannot answer your hits with Meteo or run away,
and with it broken you can finally bring it down. It still casts Meteo as it
falls, so expect to lose someone.

Thirty-seven World of Balance monsters that still used generic shields now
have shields and weak points designed for them. In each area the party you
have there carries a weapon that breaks them, and common monsters mostly
have two shields instead of three to five, so a break lands while it still
matters. Elemental weaknesses are unchanged.

Three optional fights in the World of Balance now have their own shields and
weak points instead of generic ones: the magitek guard at the Imperial Base,
the ghost in a chest on the Phantom Train, and the giant in a chest on the
Floating Continent. The giant in particular could not be broken by the usual
landing party before; blades now work on it.

The included saves were replayed on this version, so party levels and gear
differ slightly from v0.42's: a character here and there is a level up or
down, and some members carry different weapons or armor.
