## What's changed

**Cyan learns Cleave on the Phantom Train.** He learns it as his wife and
son board the train, instead of at level 70. From then on the 3-point row of
his SwdTech list is Cleave (99 MP), and the 1- and 2-point rows are his two
best other skills.

**SwdTech weak points.** Retort and Empowerer no longer chip shields.
Quadra Slice is wind: it chips wind shields and deals wind damage, so it heals monsters that absorb wind.

**Mog's Water Rondo needs all 3 Boost Points.** With fewer, the dance is
greyed out. Danced with 3 points it never stumbles, wherever the battle is.
A confused Mog no longer picks it.

**Umaro.** When he spends 3 Boost Points he always uses Storm, with or
without the Blizzard Orb. His Charge and Throw chip bludgeoning shields.

**Weak points on Mt. Zozo and the plains around Zozo.** The monsters on
Mt. Zozo, the Storm Dragon, and the monsters on the plains around Zozo,
Jidoor and Maranda now have their own weak points instead of the default
ones. Blades break most of them; Crawlers and Reach Frogs need blunt or
piercing blows, and the Mantodea has three shields. The Storm Dragon has
14 shields that blades and piercing attacks break.
