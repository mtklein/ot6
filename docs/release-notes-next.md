## What's changed

**Weak points in the Phoenix Cave.** The cave's monsters, the Red Dragon and
Doom Gaze now have their own weak points instead of the default ones, and
the cave's monsters have fewer shields (1 to 4 instead of 6). Most of them
are weak to ice, which the cave's split party may carry in only one half;
each also breaks to something the other half can bring: the Phase to holy,
the others to slashing, piercing or blunt weapons. The Red Dragon (6
shields) breaks to piercing weapons and to ice and water.

**New saves in the Phoenix Cave and after Locke joins.** The `saves` folder
has two new saves: at the Phoenix Cave's save point, with the party split,
and outside Kohlingen after the cave, with Locke in the party.

A character with a boost raised now glows with a golden aura in battle: their
outline thickens and pulses, stronger and faster at 1, 2 and 3 BP. It shows
while a command or spell list is open and stays on through the boosted action
until the BP are spent. When several characters boost at once, the one
choosing a command (or else the one boosting most) wears the full aura and
the others glow.
