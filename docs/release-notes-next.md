## What's changed

Pummel, Bum Rush and Drill play their animation once per use. Each hit
still lands on its own, with its own damage number and shield chip, one
after another once the animation ends; before, the whole animation played
again for every hit, which looked like the move being used twice.

The boost pips beside a character's name no longer flicker back to the old
count for a moment when that character's command window opens.

Each command you give Gogo now brings its owner's whole kit. Slot opens
Setzer's list (Coin Toss, Hired Help and Jackpot) instead of the bare reels.
With 3 BP his Tools fire twice (Overclock), his Runic sends spells back
(RunicBlade), his Throw kills the enemy it breaks (Assassinate), and his Fire
becomes Ultima once Terra has rejoined. With Lore and Magic both he also has
Strago's Scan, and Bserk once Strago has learned it; Scan with 3 BP studies
the enemies' lores.
His TresHnt, Painter, Blitz, SwdTech, Rage and Dance lists were already their
owners'. Morph and Leap stay Terra's and Gau's.

Relm's battle command is now called Painter, like her page in Skills. Sketch
is the first move in its list. The Fake Mustache turns Painter into Control.

## Saves

Saves from v0.28 and later load in this release. A save from before v0.44
starts with no esper progress.
