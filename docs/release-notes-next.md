## What's changed
**Aqua Rake no longer takes a lore slot.** Once Strago knows it, Aqua Rake
is in his Lore list in every battle, beside the five lores he equips.

**Strago knows Scan from the start, and Scan shows every weak point.** Scan
now marks all of the target's weak elements and weapon types at once, for
that monster and every monster of its kind, and they stay marked in later
battles. Boosted by one point or more, it reads every enemy on the field.
Celes still learns Scan at level 18.

**Strago's Scan at three Boost Points teaches him lores.** He learns every
lore the monsters in the battle could use, without being hit by them. The
lores are learned when the battle is won. It costs 99 MP. Scan aimed at
a party member reads nothing, so it costs its normal 3 MP and keeps the
Boost Points for later.

**Weak points around Jidoor, Thamasa and the Fanatics' Tower in the World
of Ruin.** The monsters in Owzer's house, on Thamasa's plain and in the
tower, and Chadarnook, the Holy Dragon and MagiMaster, now have their own
weak points instead of the default ones. Chadarnook's demon (6 shields)
breaks to blades and piercing weapons and to fire and holy; the goddess
form has none. In the tower only spells break shields: L.20 and L.70 Magic
reflect most spells, so Quake is the way through. The Holy Dragon (6) has
no weak element, so Break is the key; MagiMaster (8) is weak to whatever
element his wall shows, or to Break. Relm's brushes now also break the
Mantodea.
