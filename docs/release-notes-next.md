## What's changed

A boosted attack on one enemy now stays on that enemy. Once it falls, the
rest of the action's hits are lost instead of moving on to the next enemy,
so a boosted Fight, Pummel, Hired Help or Jackpot clears one enemy a turn.
Boost only as far as the enemy you picked needs and keep the other points
for later. Attacks that hit every enemy, like Coin Toss, keep going until
they are all down, and ones that pick at random, like Offering, still do.

Relm now supports the party. Her Sketch command opens a list, with Sketch
as its first row. Portrait gives an ally a free boost point for their next
boosted action, so two banked points and a Portrait make a divine. Tint lets
her pick an element: for four turns the ally's weapon attacks carry it, with
its weakness damage and its shield chip. Varnish makes the next enemy attack
on an ally miss. Still Life makes an enemy's break last twice as long.
Underpainting makes the next weakness hit on an enemy take two shields.
Gesture gives an ally their turn at once. Her ultimate, Masterpiece, gives
every other ally a free point. She has Portrait and Tint when she joins and
learns the rest by level and story; a boost on most rows reaches one more
ally or enemy per point. Gogo with Sketch opens her list too.

## Saves

Saves from v0.28 and later load in this release. A save from before v0.44
starts with no esper progress.
