## What's changed

Boosting a Dance now makes it less likely to stumble off its own terrain.
Without a boost it stumbles half the time, as before; with one boost point
a quarter of the time, with two one time in sixteen, and with three never.
A stumble still costs no MP, but the boost points are spent.

**Shadow's throwing kit.** Fire Skeans, Water Edges and Bolt Edges now break piercing weak points as well as their element's. A Shadow Edge or Inviz Edge thrown with boost points also covers one more party member for each point, so three points can cover a party of four. A member under Vanish can't be reached by a thrown item and is passed over. Without a boost they cover the thrower, as before.

**New weak points.** The Colosseum's own opponents (the impostor Siegfried and the Colosseum's SrBehemoth) have their own weak points.

**The Cursed Shield.** A won random battle now counts twice toward lifting the curse, so the Paladin Shield takes about half as many battles as before. Random battles come half as often in OT6, so the walking it takes is about what it was in the original game.

**A new save.** By Maranda, after Shadow joins at the Colosseum.
