## What's changed

**New weak points.** The Hoover and the Cactrot in the desert south of Maranda, and the Tyranosaur and the Brachosaur in the forest east of Gau's father's house, now have their own weak points. Blades and blunt weapons break the Hoover. The two dinosaurs shrug off blades: spears, daggers, rods and Ice break them. The Cactrot has a single shield, so the hit that breaks it also defeats it, and almost nothing but a thrown weapon reaches it.

**The Ancient Castle's monster chests.** The Master Pug in the cave's chest and the KatanaSoul in the castle's now have their own weak points: six shields each. Blades break both; Setzer's cards and dice and Relm's brushes also break the Master Pug, and spears and daggers the KatanaSoul.

**Berserk.** Siren now grants Bserk along with Mute and Sleep. Strago learns Bserk the first time he sees a monster berserk someone, as he learns lores, and keeps it as one of his spells; the Insecares on Esper Mountain can teach it, and so can the L.50 Magic in the Fanatics' Tower. He has to be standing at the end of that battle to learn it. A berserked enemy only attacks, so Bserk stops a spellcaster from casting. MagiMaster can't be silenced or put to sleep, but he can be berserked: he stops casting and stops changing his weak point, though his final spell still comes when he falls. The catch is that his weak point stays as it was when Bserk landed, so berserk him while it is one your party can hit, or he is left with a weak point nobody can reach while he attacks hard.

**A battle freeze.** A battle could freeze for good when a character had the Magic list open while a long animation played, such as a spell bouncing off several reflecting party members. This no longer happens.

**More gear in the World of Balance saves.** The saves now pick up the gear a player finds along the way: the Ribbon, Hyper Wrist and RunningShoes in the cellar under South Figaro on Locke's escape, the Atlas Armlet on Mt. Kolts, the Hero Ring in the house north of Kohlingen, and the RunningShoes in Zozo's rooftop room. The chests in the Narshe mines and South Figaro's town chests are left closed in the World of Balance, because their World of Ruin versions hold better prizes.

**More treasure in the World of Ruin saves.** The World of Ruin saves now carry what a player collects on the way: the chests of the towns, Zozo, Owzer's basement, the Tzen house, Doma's dream and castle, the Ancient Castle and the Zone Eater, among them the Moogle Suit, Genji Armor, Zephyr Cape and a second Hero Ring, plus South Figaro's town chests and the Pod Bracelet and Elixir in the Moogle cave. Strago learns GrandTrain from Hidon at Ebot's Rock, so the saves from there on have it. The party also levels up on the plains on the way to the Colosseum, and every Ribbon it owns is worn.

**Two new saves.** One by the dinosaur forest, after levelling the party there, and one by Maranda, after the Cactrot desert, the last stop before Kefka's Tower.
