# Next release

Add each player-visible change as one short paragraph. Describe the change
and its effect. Cite evidence in the commit message.

## What's changed

**Terra's Morph works at any boost.** Boost Points set how long it lasts:
with 0 it lasts about two of her turns, longer with 1 or 2, and with 3 it
lasts until the battle ends or she reverts. The morph gauge that carried
over between battles is gone, so Morph is never greyed out. A confused
Terra's Morph lasts the shortest time.

**Revert no longer spends Boost Points.** A boosted Revert used to spend
the points and gain nothing. Now the points stay banked, and Terra gains one
as on any unboosted turn.

**Weak points around Narshe in the World of Ruin.** The monsters in the
town, the mines, the cliffs, the moogle cave and Umaro's cave, and the Ice
Dragon, Tritoch and Umaro, now have their own weak points instead of the
default ones. Test Riders break only to piercing weapons. The Ice Dragon (6
shields) breaks to piercing and blunt weapons, Tritoch (10) and Umaro (6) to
blades and piercing weapons, and all three to fire.

**The Flame, Ice and Thunder Shields no longer teach spells.** They taught
Fire 2, Ice 2 and Bolt 2. Those come from boosting Fire, Ice and Bolt, as
every stronger spell does.

**Aqua Rake no longer takes a lore slot.** Once Strago knows it, Aqua Rake
is in his Lore list in every battle, beside the five lores he equips.

**Strago knows Scan from the start, and Scan shows every weak point.** Scan
now marks all of the target's weak elements and weapon types at once, for
that monster and every monster of its kind, and they stay marked in later
battles. Boosted by one point or more, it reads every enemy on the field.
Celes still learns Scan at level 18.
