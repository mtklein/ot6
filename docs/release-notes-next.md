## What's changed

**Weak points in the Veldt cave.** Toe Cutters, Rhyos, the Allo Ver in the
cave's treasure chest and the SrBehemoth have their own weak points. Blunt
weapons and Gau's bare fists break all four. Toe Cutters and the Allo Ver
have 3 shields and Rhyos 4 (6, 4 and 6 before); the SrBehemoth keeps 6.
Toe Cutters and the SrBehemoth still absorb ice.

**A second weak point for fourteen early monsters.** Were-Rats, Leafers,
Vomammoths, Dark Winds, M-TekArmors, Repo Men, Areneids, Stray Cats, Beakors,
CrassHopprs, Bombs, Ghosts, Rhobites and Popliums each gain a second weak
point, which also applies when they come on the Veldt. Their shields are
unchanged.

**New saves at the Veldt cave and after Gau's father.** The `saves` folder
has two new saves: at the Veldt cave's save point, with Gau in the party,
and beside the airship by Duncan's house, after the cave, Gau's father and
Sabin's Bum Rush.
