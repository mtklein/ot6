## What's changed

**Kefka's final battle has its own shields and weak points.** Every part of the last fight now has shields and weapon weaknesses chosen for it, from the arms and face at the bottom to Kefka at the top. Each tier between its parts can be broken by every kind of weapon (blades, spears and knives, rods and clubs, dice, cards and brushes), so whichever four you send first hold a key in every tier, and Kefka himself can be broken by all of them. Spells heal the lady in the third tier, so only weapons break her, and while she is broken she can't bring the sleeper back.
