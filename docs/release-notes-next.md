## What's changed

**Ribbons stay with the party in the World of Ruin saves.** When the party changes members on the Falcon, anyone left behind now hands over their Ribbon first, and it goes on someone in the new party. Before, a Ribbon could stay on a character waiting on the airship, and the party reached the Colosseum and Maranda with only one of its three Ribbons on the members it was using.

**A new save before Kefka's Tower, with the reserve members trained.** The last save before the tower used to leave eight characters where they were when they last fought, some as low as level 32. A new save after it has them trained on the plain by Maranda two at a time: everyone is now at level 40 or higher, and Sabin and Gogo are at 48. Terra and Celes stay at 64 and 63.
