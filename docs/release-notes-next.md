## What's changed

**New weak points.** The Hoover and the Cactrot in the desert south of Maranda, and the Tyranosaur and the Brachosaur in the forest north of Gau's father's house, now have their own weak points. Blades break the Hoover. The two dinosaurs shrug off blades: spears, daggers, rods and Ice break them. The Cactrot has a single shield, so the hit that breaks it also defeats it, and almost nothing but a thrown weapon reaches it.

**Two new saves.** One by the dinosaur forest, after levelling the party there, and one by Maranda, after the Cactrot desert, the last stop before Kefka's Tower.

A battle could freeze for good, with an animation stuck on screen, when a
character had Magic or another ability list open while a long animation was
playing, such as a spell bouncing off several reflecting party members.
This no longer happens.

**Berserk.** Siren now grants Bserk along with Mute and Sleep. Strago learns Bserk the first time he sees a monster berserk someone, as he learns lores, and keeps it as one of his spells; the Insecares on Esper Mountain can teach it, and so can the magic users in the Fanatics' Tower. He has to be standing at the end of that battle to learn it. A berserked enemy only attacks, so Bserk stops a spellcaster from casting. MagiMaster can't be silenced or put to sleep, but he can be berserked: he stops casting and stops changing his weak point, though his final spell still comes when he falls. Gau's rages and Odin offer Bserk as before.
