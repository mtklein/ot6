## What's changed

**Kefka's final battle has its own shields and weak points.** Every part of the last fight now has shields and weapon weaknesses chosen for it, from the arms and face at the bottom to Kefka at the top. Each tier between its parts can be broken by every kind of weapon (blades, spears and knives, rods and clubs, dice, cards and brushes), so whichever four you send first hold a key in every tier, and Kefka himself can be broken by all of them. The lady in the third tier is the exception: spells heal her, and only blades, spears and knives break her.

**The saves were replayed on this version.** Every save in the `saves` folder was made again by playing the story on v0.40, so levels, gil and items differ a little from v0.39's. The last one, ready for Kefka, now carries the two Barrier Rings the party buys in Thamasa before the tower.
