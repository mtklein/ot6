## What's changed

**New weak points.** The Hoover and the Cactrot in the desert south of Maranda, and the Tyranosaur and the Brachosaur in the forest north of Gau's father's house, now have their own weak points. Blades break the Hoover. The two dinosaurs shrug off blades: spears, daggers, rods and Ice break them. The Cactrot has a single shield, so the hit that breaks it also defeats it, and almost nothing but a thrown weapon reaches it.

**The Ancient Castle's monster chests.** The Master Pug in the cave's chest and the KatanaSoul in the castle's now have their own weak points: six shields each. Blades break both; Setzer's cards and dice and Relm's brushes also break the Master Pug, and spears and daggers the KatanaSoul.

**More gear in the bundled saves.** The included saves now pick up the World of Balance gear a player finds on the way: the Ribbon, Hyper Wrist and RunningShoes in the cellar under South Figaro on Locke's escape, the Atlas Armlet on Mt. Kolts, the Hero Ring in the house north of Kohlingen, and the RunningShoes in Zozo's tower room. The chests in the Narshe mines and South Figaro's town chests are left closed in the World of Balance, because opening them there empties the better prizes their World of Ruin versions hold; the World of Ruin saves pick up South Figaro's and, in the Moogle cave, the Pod Bracelet and the Elixir.

**Two new saves.** One by the dinosaur forest, after levelling the party there, and one by Maranda, after the Cactrot desert, the last stop before Kefka's Tower.

A battle could freeze for good, with an animation stuck on screen, when a
character had Magic or another ability list open while a long animation was
playing, such as a spell bouncing off several reflecting party members.
This no longer happens.

**Berserk.** Siren now grants Bserk along with Mute and Sleep. Strago learns Bserk the first time he sees a monster berserk someone, as he learns lores, and keeps it as one of his spells; the Insecares on Esper Mountain can teach it, and so can the L.50 Magic in the Fanatics' Tower. He has to be standing at the end of that battle to learn it. A berserked enemy only attacks, so Bserk stops a spellcaster from casting. MagiMaster can't be silenced or put to sleep, but he can be berserked: he stops casting and stops changing his weak point, though his final spell still comes when he falls. The catch is that his weak point stays as it was when Bserk landed, so berserk him while it is one your party can hit, or he is left with a weak point nobody can reach while he attacks hard.
