## What's changed

**Ribbons stay with the party in the World of Ruin saves.** When the party changes members on the Falcon, anyone left behind now hands over their Ribbon first, and it goes on someone in the new party. Before, a Ribbon could stay on a character waiting on the airship, and the party reached the Colosseum and Maranda with only one of its three Ribbons on the members it was using.

**A new save before Kefka's Tower, with the reserve members trained.** In the last save before the tower, seven characters were still at the levels they had when they left the party, some as low as 32. A new save after it trains them two at a time on the plain by Maranda, beside Shadow and Cyan: everyone is now at level 40 or higher, Sabin and Gogo are at 48, and Shadow and Cyan rise from 53 to 61. Terra and Celes stay at 64 and 63.
