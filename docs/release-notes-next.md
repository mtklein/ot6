## What's changed

Four World of Balance monsters off the story's path now have shields designed
for them: the Bug and the ChickenLip in the forests by Albrook, the Grenade on
the bomb isle, and the Intangir on Triangle Island. The Grenade explodes when
it is hit with Fight, so its shields break to Edgar's tools and Sabin's
blitzes instead. The Intangir absorbs every element, but any weapon breaks it,
Setzer's cards and dice included, and while it is broken it cannot cast Meteo
or run away.
